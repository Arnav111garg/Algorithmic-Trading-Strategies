# Algorithmic Trading Strategies

A quantitative research project exploring systematic trading strategies, alpha generation, financial data engineering, market microstructure, machine learning, portfolio construction, and execution algorithms.

## Overview

**Algorithmic Trading Strategies** is a structured research and development project focused on understanding the principles, models, and methodologies behind systematic trading.

The project explores the complete quantitative research lifecycle, from identifying potential market inefficiencies and developing alpha models to portfolio construction, risk management, backtesting, and execution.

It combines financial mathematics, statistical modelling, data analysis, and computational methods to study how systematic trading strategies are researched, evaluated, and implemented.

## Key Research Areas

### 1. Alpha Research & Strategy Development
Exploring systematic approaches to identifying market patterns, formulating research hypotheses, and studying potential sources of alpha.

### 2. Quantitative Trading Strategies
Studying different classes of systematic strategies, including momentum, mean reversion, statistical arbitrage, and cross-sectional factor investing.

### 3. Market Microstructure & Order Flow
Understanding market structure, order flow dynamics, liquidity, price formation, and their relevance to short-horizon quantitative research.

### 4. Machine Learning for Alpha Research
Exploring machine learning methodologies for identifying patterns, modelling financial data, and evaluating potential predictive signals.

### 5. Portfolio Construction & Risk Management
Studying portfolio optimization, capital allocation, risk measurement, exposure management, and portfolio-level performance evaluation.

### 6. Backtesting & Execution
Understanding systematic strategy evaluation, realistic simulation, transaction costs, execution algorithms, and the challenges of translating research into practical systems.

## Research Methodology

The project follows a structured quantitative research framework:

1. **Research:** Identify a market phenomenon and formulate a testable hypothesis.
2. **Data Engineering:** Organize, process, and validate relevant financial data.
3. **Modelling:** Develop mathematical, statistical, or computational models.
4. **Strategy Research:** Investigate systematic approaches and potential alpha signals.
5. **Evaluation:** Study performance, robustness, and out-of-sample behaviour.
6. **Risk Analysis:** Examine exposures, drawdowns, and portfolio-level risks.
7. **Execution:** Understand the relationship between theoretical performance and realistic execution.

## Project Objectives

- Develop a strong foundation in quantitative and algorithmic trading.
- Understand the mathematical and statistical principles behind systematic strategies.
- Explore different approaches to alpha research and signal development.
- Study the intersection of machine learning and quantitative finance.
- Understand portfolio-level optimization and risk management.
- Develop a research-oriented understanding of backtesting and execution systems.

## Philosophy

**Research before execution. Evidence before assumptions. Robustness before complexity.**

The emphasis is on understanding why a model works, evaluating whether its results are statistically meaningful, and recognizing the limitations of quantitative models.

## Disclaimer

This repository is intended exclusively for educational and quantitative research purposes. The research and models discussed here should not be interpreted as financial or investment advice. No strategy or model is guaranteed to generate profits.
